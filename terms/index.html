2. What UIMI is

UIMI lets you record yourself practising a conversation and gives you AI-written feedback estimating how you likely came across.

Please understand what that means:

  • The feedback is generated by an AI model. It is an estimate, not a fact, and it can be wrong, incomplete, or miss the point.
  • UIMI does not know what any real person thought or felt about you. It only reads what you said and how you said it.
  • UIMI is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, mental-health treatment, legal advice, or career advice. It is practice. Do not use it as a substitute for a professional.
  • We do not promise any outcome. UIMI cannot promise you will get the job, pass the interview, win the argument, make the friend, or be better liked. Nobody can promise that.

If you are struggling or feel unsafe, please talk to someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988 at any time. Outside the US, findahelpline.com lists free services near you. If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number. UIMI is not a crisis service and cannot help in an emergency.

We do not use your recordings to train AI models, and we do not share them with other users. See the Privacy Policy for who processes your data on our behalf.

Only record yourself. Do not record another person without their knowledge and permission. Recording other people may be illegal where you live, and it is your responsibility, not ours.
