FAQ

Questions.

What is UIMI?

A private practice room for real conversations. Pick a situation, say your response out loud, and see how you likely came across, with one thing to improve before you try again. An AI model transcribes what you said and reads your words and delivery against the situation and the impression you were going for. It's an estimate, not a verdict.

Who is UIMI for?

Students, from high school through college, who want to feel ready for the conversations that matter: interviews, class, presentations, and the everyday ones that feel awkward. You can also paste your own speech or describe the conversation you're about to have. UIMI is for ages 13 and up.

Are my recordings private?

Audio only. No camera, no audience. Yes. Only your account can play them back. They're never shared with other people, never sold, and never used to train AI models. A parent who pays does not get access. You can delete any practice, or your whole account, right in the app.

Is UIMI therapy?

No. UIMI is a practice tool, like rehearsing with a coach. It isn't counselling or mental health care, and it isn't a crisis service. If someone is struggling or feels unsafe, in the US they can call or text 988 at any time.

When is UIMI launching?

UIMI is coming to iPhone first. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as it's ready.

Is UIMI free?

Your first three practice sessions are free. Premium, for unlimited practice, will be available at launch, with prices shown clearly before anyone pays.
