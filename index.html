FAQ

Questions.

How does it work if I'm paying for my teen?

You subscribe, then send your teen an invite link. They set up their own practice room on their phone. You never need to log in to their account, and you can cancel any time from yours.

Can I see how my teen is doing?

You'll see how many times they've practiced and which arenas they've used. You won't see recordings, transcripts, or feedback. That stays with them, so they'll actually use it.

What is UIMI?

A private practice room for real conversations. Pick a situation, say your response out loud, and see how you likely came across, with one thing to improve before you try again. An AI model transcribes what you said and reads your words and delivery against the situation and the impression you were going for. It's an estimate, not a verdict.

Who is UIMI for?

Teens and young adults, from high school through college, who want to feel ready for the conversations that matter: interviews, class, presentations, and the everyday ones that feel awkward. Parents subscribe; teens practice. UIMI is for ages 13 and up.

Are my recordings private?

Audio only. No camera, no audience. Yes. Only your account can play them back. They're never shared with other people, never sold, and never used to train AI models. Parents see practice counts, never recordings or transcripts. You can delete any practice, or your whole account, right in the app.

Is UIMI therapy?

No. UIMI is a practice tool, like rehearsing with a coach. It isn't counselling or mental health care, and it isn't a crisis service. If someone is struggling or feels unsafe, in the US they can call or text 988 at any time.
