Questions

Good questions to ask.

Who is UIMI for?

High school students, and anyone 13 or older, who want to get better at the conversations that matter: interviews, presentations, speaking up in class, asking for what they need, and the everyday ones that feel awkward.

How does the feedback work?

You say your response out loud. An AI model transcribes it and reads what you said and how you said it, against the situation and the impression you were going for. You get one likely reading, what worked, what got in the way, and one move to try next. It's an estimate, not a verdict, and it never scores you.

Is it therapy?

No. UIMI is a practice tool, like rehearsing with a coach. It isn't counselling or mental health care, and it isn't a crisis service. If someone is struggling or feels unsafe, in the US they can call or text 988 at any time.

Who can hear my recordings?

Only you. Recordings are private to your account, never sold, never shown to other people, and never used to train AI models. A parent who pays does not get access. You can delete any practice, or your whole account, whenever you want.

What does it cost?

Your first three practice sessions are free. Premium, for unlimited practice, will be available when UIMI launches, and prices will be shown clearly before anyone pays.
